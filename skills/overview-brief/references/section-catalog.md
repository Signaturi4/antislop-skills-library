# Section catalog

Ten sections. Each: purpose, what to pull, component, headline pattern. Skip a section when no source feeds it; never fill it with generic text.

| # | Section | Purpose | Pull from | Component | Headline pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Why we exist | the belief and the long game | manifesto axioms, position, closing line | 3 cards + ladder + pull quote | "The next decade belongs to ..." |
| 2 | What we sell today | the cash engine now | offer, product one-liner, services | 2 cards + metaphor quote | "A working product, plus ..." |
| 3 | Who buys and why | evidence about buyers | research findings with numbers and scope | table: finding -> what we do | the top finding as a sentence |
| 4 | The story we tell | villain, hero, guide, pillars | brand story core table, pillars, words | two numbered lists + belief quote | "The enemy is a habit: ..." |
| 5 | How we are organised | humans, agents, the open seat | team board roles + KPIs, agent model | 3 role cards + agents card + open seat | "Three roles, one funnel, ..." |
| 6 | Unit economics | how the money works | funnel, margin, rate, price ladder, targets | funnel bars + key-value table + ladder table | "Interviews close, volume is the gap" |
| 7 | Go-to-market | next period plan | channel plan, weekly numbers, blockers | 3 channel cards + scoreboard table | "Sell on X, deliver through Y, ..." |
| 8 | Infra and DevOps | what already runs | IaC repo, server notes, gates, open PRs | SVG diagram + status table + change path | "Everything is code, everything risky waits for a yes" |
| 9 | How we work | principles inside and with clients | rules files, manifesto, never-share list | two-column list | "The principles we hold" |
| 10 | First steps | where the reader starts | reading list, access, rituals, open decisions | ordered list | "Where to start" |

Audience variants drop sections (see SKILL.md step 1). For a single project instead of a company: 1 becomes "Why this project", 5 becomes "Who works on it", 6 becomes "Budget and hours".
